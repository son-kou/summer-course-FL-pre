## Use In This Course

The image is used as an educational public MRI visual for illustrating apparent domain shift through browser-side display transformations. The transformations are conceptual teaching approximations, not scanner physics, segmentation ground truth, or evidence from Pati et al. or FeTS.

No claim is made that this image is part of any federated-learning study, FeTS challenge data, or data contributed by the presenter.
